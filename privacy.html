1. What the app does

Resume Banao lets you type your details (name, phone, email, work history, education, skills) and shows a resume you can save as a PDF.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. We have no accounts, no sign-in and no server that receives what you type.

3. Where your data is stored

What you enter is saved only on your own device (in the app's local storage) so it is still there when you come back. The PDF you create is saved by you, on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes it. We cannot see or recover it.

4. Third-party services

To load fonts and the PDF tools, the app fetches files from Google Fonts and the cdnjs content network. Like any website, these services can see your IP address and device type when the files are requested. They do not receive the details you type into your resume.

5. Advertising

This version of the app shows no ads. If ads are added in a future version, this policy will be updated first and will explain what the ad provider collects.

6. Children

The app is meant for job seekers and is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
